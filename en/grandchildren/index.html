An Advent gift for your grandchildren, even when they live far away

The Journey with Luke is an online Advent calendar game built on the Christmas story in Luke’s Gospel. Your grandchild becomes the apprentice of Luke the physician and solves one short case a day, 1–24 December. For ages 8 and up, well into the teens. The code comes by email with a gift card as a PDF, so you can send it to a home far away.

Something to share, even at a distance

When a door is solved, an adult gets two questions to talk about around the table. If you are not at the same table, ask your grandchild which door they have reached and what they found out. Grown-ups who play along have just as much fun.

When to give it

Before 1 December

Give the gift card on the first Sunday of Advent, 29 November. Then everything is ready when the calendar starts on Tuesday 1 December.

During December

If the code is given in the middle of December, the doors up to and including that day are open at once, and a new door opens each day through to Christmas Eve. The child can catch up when it suits.

Under the tree

After Christmas Eve all 24 doors are open, and the game stays open at least until 31 January 2027. So the code can also be a Christmas present, played through the holidays.

Questions and answers

Can we give a code to a child in another home?

Yes. The gift card with the code comes as a PDF with the email. Does the child live somewhere else? Send the PDF by email or message to the parents. They can print it, or scan the QR code straight from the screen. Share the card only with the person the gift is for. If you give to children, grandchildren or someone you know in several homes, each home needs its own code.

What age is it for?

The game is for ages 8 and up, well into the teens. Younger players can take part together with an adult or an older sibling, and all the cards can be read aloud.

Is it only for Christian families?

The game is made on a Christian foundation and takes the Bible seriously. At the same time it practises something everyone can use: asking good questions and testing what you hear. Everyone is welcome to take part.

What if we buy in the middle of December?

Then the doors up to and including today’s date are open at once, and a new door opens each day through to Christmas Eve. You can catch up with the days when it suits you.

Does it work on a phone, a tablet and a computer?

Yes. The game runs in the browser on a phone, a tablet and a computer, with nothing to install. A new door needs the internet the first time it is opened. After that it can be played offline on the same device. On iPhone and iPad, iOS 16.4 or later is needed.
