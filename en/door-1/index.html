Door 1 of a Christian Advent calendar for kids

The Journey with Luke is a Bible advent game and online Christian Advent calendar for kids, built on the Christmas story in Luke’s Gospel. Children become the apprentice of Luke the physician and solve one short case a day, 1–24 December. Door 1 is free, with no account and nothing to install.

Open door 1 in the game Buy a code for doors 2–24

The game runs in the browser on a phone, a tablet or a computer. No in-app purchases, no ads and no tracking in the game.

What happens in door 1

In door 1 you are in Caesarea. You talk with people, look for clues and decide what has happened – the same way as in the other doors. The day’s task usually takes 5–15 minutes.

When door 1 is done, an adult can buy a personal code (€24.99 / £19.99 / $24.99) or a family code (€49.99 / £39.99 / $49.99) to unlock doors 2–24. Prices are on the front page and in checkout.

More about the calendar

  • 24 doors, 1–24 December. After Christmas Eve all doors are open, and the game stays open at least until 31 January 2027.
  • Ages 8 and up, into the teens. Grown-ups who play along enjoy it too.
  • Free activity sheets for every day are at Activities.
