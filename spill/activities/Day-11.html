Read the tasks as text
Picture descriptions for the tasks:
1. A path divides the picture in two: the area BY THE TREE on the left, BY THE GATE on the right. A by the tree has one spot. B by the tree has two spots. C by the gate has two spots. D by the gate has no spots. The message says: The sheep has two spots on its back. It is by the tree. Choose the sheep that fits both clues.
2. The cards from the left: She finds the sheep. She tells Mika. She hears the message. The drawings show Mira with a sheep by a tree, Mira talking to Mika and Mira listening to a message about the sheep and the tree. Each card has an empty circle for the number.
3. Another flock: four sheep on the left card and two on the right. The cards say There are four sheep here. and There are two sheep here. Answer choices: Six sheep, Four sheep.
4. A big empty frame with some grass in two corners.

Draw a sheep on a small piece of paper and hide it. Give a friend one clue about the place. Give
another clue if needed. Swap roles. Hide the paper where you can both reach it safely.
