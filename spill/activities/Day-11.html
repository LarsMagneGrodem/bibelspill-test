The shepherds and the angels
Door 11 in the game. Read it yourself in the Bible: Luke 2:8–20.

3 From Luke
Some shepherds were living out in the fields nearby, keeping watch over their flock at night. An angel of the Lord appeared to them and said, “Do not be afraid.” In the town of David a Saviour had been born. Suddenly a great company of the heavenly host was with the angel, praising God: glory to God in the highest, and peace on earth (Luke 2:8–14).
How would the shepherds know the child? Write the words from the word bank in the right places. The angel said, “You will find a (1) … wrapped in (2) … and lying in a (3) ….” When the angels had left, the shepherds said, “Let us go to (4) ….” Word bank: manger, baby, cloths, Bethlehem. Draw the night sky over the fields.

4 Detective: today’s clues
Today’s check: There were shepherds out in the fields near Bethlehem, guarding their sheep at night (Luke 2:8). The historians say: Strong clues. Here is why: People have kept sheep around Bethlehem for thousands of years. King David was a shepherd boy there, and the prophet Amos was a sheep farmer in nearby Tekoa.
Which king was a shepherd boy near Bethlehem long before? King Herod / The emperor Augustus / King David
Extra challenge: Read 1 Samuel 16:11–13. Where was David when the prophet Samuel came to find a new king?

5 Together
Talk together: The angels came to shepherds out in the fields at night, not to people in a palace. What do you think about that?
Do together: Go outside one evening and look at the sky. What would it be like to look after sheep outdoors all night?
