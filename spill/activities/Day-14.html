Simeon in the temple
Door 14 in the game. Read it yourself in the Bible: Luke 2:22–35.

3 From Luke
Mary and Joseph took the child to the temple in Jerusalem and offered a sacrifice, as the law says: two doves. There they met Simeon, who was waiting for God to comfort his people. He took the child in his arms and praised God: now he had seen God’s salvation, a light for the nations (Luke 2:22–32).
Write the answers in the boxes. The letters in the thick boxes spell the name of a man, from top to bottom. 1. The city where the temple stood (9). 2. Mary and Joseph brought the … to the temple (5). 3. Simeon took the child in his … (4). 4. The birds that were offered (5). 5. He went with Mary to the temple (6). 6. A light for the … (7).

4 Detective: today’s clues
Today’s check: The family could not afford a lamb, so they offered two doves (Luke 2:22–24). The historians say: Good clues. Here is why: Luke names the offering the law gives to those who cannot afford a lamb. That fits with Joseph being an ordinary craftsman.
The law said: a lamb, or two doves for those who cannot afford a lamb. What does the offering tell us about the family? That they had little money / That they were rich / That they owned many sheep
Extra challenge: Look up Leviticus 12:8. What does the law say there?

5 Together
Talk together: Simeon had waited a long time. What did he say when he saw the child?
Do together: Simeon waited a long time. Tell about something you have waited a long time for. What was it like when it finally happened?
