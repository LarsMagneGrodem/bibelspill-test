Picture descriptions for the tasks:
1. Mira’s plan: Pointed roof. Round hole. Two stripes on the wall. House A has a pointed roof, a square hole and two stripes. House B has a pointed roof, a round hole and two stripes. House C has a flat roof, a round hole and two stripes. Circle or tell which one fits.
2. The cards from the top: 1 I saw two birds by the tree. 2 I hope a bird moves in. 3 I put up the house today. On the right are the boxes HAS HAPPENED and HOPE. Connect or tell.
3. A drawing shows a bird on the roof. The inside of the house is not shown. Mira says I hope three baby birds will come. Noah says I saw one bird on the roof. Answer choices: Three baby birds. The cards do not say.
4. An empty drawing frame with a star and a leaf in the corners.
