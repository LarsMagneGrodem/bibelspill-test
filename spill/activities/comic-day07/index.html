Read the tasks and picture descriptions as text
On page 1 the merchant in green gives the boy in blue-green, with a brown bag on a strap across him, a blank note. In the counting picture below there are two baskets on the counter and one underneath. One jar stands on the counter, another underneath. Three apples lie on the right under the counter. The work cards from the left say Write the number, Check once more and Count the baskets. On page 2 the boy points at a blank note while the merchant watches. The two text cards below are note A: Count all the baskets, and note B: I have counted three baskets. Each card has a tick box. At the bottom is an empty stall to draw five things in, and a note with space for the number. The pictures and messages are made up.
