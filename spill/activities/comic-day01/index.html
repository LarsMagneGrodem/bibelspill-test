Read the tasks and picture descriptions as text
The picture on page 1 shows a donkey tied with a rope to a post, a cart on its side, a damaged wheel and broken jars in front of the cart. Two whole jars stand right by the wheel. The jars on the stall in the background should not be counted. Below the picture is a practice wheel with a long opening and a small notch on the top. A is triangular. B is long with a small notch on the top. C has a notch on the left side. On page 2 Lea, in purple, points towards the cart, while Mika, in orange, walks up to the place. Both pictures show the cart after the accident, not the moment it tipped over. The statements are under the pictures. Lea says that she saw the cart tip over. Mika says that he came afterwards. The drawing area holds an empty cart with two wheels, which the child can fill with three jars and something that keeps them steady. The shape task needs sight, or help to describe the shapes.
