Lea has dark wavy hair and purple clothes. Mika has brown curls and an orange tunic. On page 1 the door in the middle is fully closed; the inside is not shown. Three jars stand on the left. One basket stands on the right. One bird sits high up on the wall on each side of the door. Below the picture is an empty box for the number of jars and two questions with tick boxes. On page 2 Lea says: I stood outside. I saw three jars by the door. Mika says: I was inside the room. In there I saw a lamp. Both statements are visible. Below them is an empty drawing frame for three things of your own and an optional guessing game with a version for one. This is a made-up courtyard, not a reconstruction of a particular temple.
