Three titles
Door 12 in the game. Read it yourself in the Bible: Luke 2:11.

3 From Luke
The angel told the shepherds that a Saviour had been born in the town of David. “He is the Messiah, the Lord” (Luke 2:11). The child is given three titles at once. All three belong to the same child.
Each sign is a letter. Use the key to find the three titles the angel gave the child. 7 signs: one who rescues. 7 signs: the anointed one. 4 signs: a word also used for God.

4 Detective: today’s clues
Today’s check: Many Jews in Jesus’ time were waiting for a Messiah, a king from David’s family (Luke 1:32–33; 2:11, 25). The historians say: Strong clues. Here is why: Jewish writings from just before Jesus’ time look forward to such a king, among them the Psalms of Solomon and the Dead Sea Scrolls. The historian Josephus also tells of the hope.
What kind of king were many Jews waiting for? An emperor from Rome / A king from David’s family / A king from Egypt
Extra challenge: Read Luke 1:32–33. Whose throne will the child be given?

5 Together
Talk together: People expected different things of the Messiah. What do you think they hoped for?
Do together: What “titles” do you have? Sister, goalkeeper, friend? Write three on a slip of paper, and let the others guess who it is about.
