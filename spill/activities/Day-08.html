The road to Bethlehem
Door 8 in the game. Read it yourself in the Bible: Luke 2:4–5.

3 From Luke
Joseph went from the town of Nazareth in Galilee up to Judea, to the town of David, which is called Bethlehem. Mary went with him, and she was expecting a child (Luke 2:4–5). Luke does not say which road they took. He tells where they started and where they arrived.
Find a way from Nazareth to Bethlehem. You cannot go through the walls. NAZARETH – BETHLEHEM.

4 Detective: today’s clues
Today’s check: From Nazareth in Galilee Joseph went “up” to Bethlehem, the town of David in Judea (Luke 2:4). The historians say: Strong clues. Here is why: Bethlehem lies high in the hills south of Jerusalem, much higher than Nazareth. The historian Josephus writes that the quick road from Galilee to Jerusalem took three days.
What is in Luke 2:4–5? Put one cross on each line. Luke says it / Luke does not say it: They set out from Nazareth. Mary rode on a donkey. They went up to Bethlehem.
Extra challenge: Josephus writes that the quick road from Galilee to Jerusalem took three days. Bethlehem lies a little further south. What can we work out about the journey?

5 Together
Talk together: The text says Joseph and Mary went from Nazareth to Bethlehem, but not which way they went. What do we do when a text does not tell us everything?
Do together: Go for a walk together. Afterwards, say only where you started and where you ended. Can anyone guess the way you went?
