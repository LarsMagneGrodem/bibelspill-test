Day 8 · Five optional activities. Get a pencil and some colours, and choose one task at a time.

Read the tasks as text
Picture descriptions for the tasks:
1. Lea’s house is on the left and Mika’s house on the right. Two roads link the houses. The upper one goes past the woods. The lower one goes past the well. Draw or describe both possibilities.
2. A landscape seen from the side: the hut stands high on the hill to the left. The bridge crosses the water down in the valley to the right. Which place is lowest? Draw or describe an arrow from the hut down to the bridge.
4. A blank postcard: a drawing space on the left, To: and From: lines and a leaf stamp on the right.
