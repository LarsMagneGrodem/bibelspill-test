Look and listen by the river

Day 17 · Five optional activities. Get a pencil and some colours, and choose one task at a time.

Download both sheets · PDFAnswers for adults · PDF
Read the tasks as text
Picture descriptions for the tasks:
1. Both pictures show a river, a boat, a jetty, a tree and ducks. First picture: a triangular flag, three jetty posts, two ducks and three apples. Second picture: a square flag, two jetty posts, three ducks and one apple. Find four differences.
2. The cards from the top: I heard a splash. I saw a boat. I heard a duck. On the right are an eye with SAW and an ear with HEARD. Connect or tell what fits.
3. Lea says I saw the boat come in to the bank. Mika says I heard a splash behind the reeds. I did not see the boat. Who says they saw the boat? Answer choices: Lea. Mika.
4. A drawing frame with reeds along the sides. Make up one possible cause of the splash and draw it.

What could have made the splash?
Mika does not say what made the sound. Make up one idea and draw it behind the reeds.
This is your idea, not something the card tells us.
5
Guess a sound
Make a sound with your hands while a friend closes their eyes. Let your friend guess what you did.
Show it afterwards. What did your friend hear, and what did your friend guess? Swap roles.
