Read the tasks and picture descriptions as text
The picture on page 1 shows one oil lamp on a table on the left, two chairs on the right, a basket on the floor and an open window. Lea says that she saw a lamp. Mika says that he saw two chairs. On page 2 they come in together on another evening. Lea says two lamps, Mika says three. The drawings show the children talking, but not the inside of the room on this second evening. The children look thoughtful; their faces do not decide who remembers correctly. At the bottom is an empty frame for your own room.
