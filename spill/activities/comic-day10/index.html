Read the tasks and picture descriptions as text
The practice house on page 1 has two mats and an oil lamp on the left, and a box of hay on the right. The cards below say SLEEP, FOOD FOR ANIMALS and LIGHT. The note in task 2 says: In the room there were two mats and a lamp. Drawing A has two mats on the left, a lamp on the right and a round window. B has two mats at the bottom, the lamp above them and a square window. The answer options are Only A, Only B and Both. On page 2 the new note says: The guest slept on a mat. Lea wants to draw a cat; Mika asks whether the cat is on the note. Answer options: A MAT or A CAT. Luke says that we do not know if there was a cat, because the note says nothing about it. Below the conversation is an empty room frame for your own drawing. The pictures are made up and do not show a particular historical house.
