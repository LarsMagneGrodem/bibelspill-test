Read the tasks as text
Picture descriptions for the tasks:
1. The room has one lamp on a table on the left and two chairs on the right. A basket stands on the floor to the left. A window shows stars. Under the picture: Lea says I saw a lamp. Mika says I saw two chairs. Draw lines or tell which things each child mentions.
2. The two answer choices you can see are: Yes. A room can have both a lamp and two chairs. No. They must mention exactly the same things.
3. Another evening, Ida and Teo came in together. Two new notes: Ida says Two lamps shone as we came in. Teo says Three lamps shone as we came in. Under Ida’s text are two lamp signs; under Teo’s are three. Find the number word on each note. Answer choices: How many lamps were lit. What colour the lamps were.
4. A big empty drawing frame with decorations in the corners.

We must ask more before we pick a number. Different numbers alone do not show who remembers correctly.

Two explorers
Look at the drawing with a friend. Choose one thing each. Say what you see without saying its name.
Let the other person point. Swap roles. The picture can stay out the whole time.
