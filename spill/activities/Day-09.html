3 From Luke
The shepherds said to one another, “Let us go to Bethlehem.” They hurried there and found Mary and Joseph, and the baby lying in the manger. They told what they had heard about the child. Then they went back, praising God for all they had heard and seen (Luke 2:15–20).
It is dark in Bethlehem, and the letters are in a muddle. Put them in the right order. The town where Jesus was born: EEHLTMHEB. They watched sheep at night: EPHRSHSED. Where the baby lay: ANEMRG. The baby’s mother: YRAM.

4 Detective: today’s clues
Today’s check: Jesus was born in Bethlehem (Luke 2:4–7). The historians say: Good clues. Here is why: Some scholars think the place came from the prophet Micah. But Matthew and Luke both say Bethlehem, each in his own account, and no source says anything else.
Which two Gospels tell, each in its own account, that Jesus was born in Bethlehem? Matthew and Luke / Only Luke / Mark and John
Extra challenge: Read Micah 5:2 (5:1 in some Bibles). Which town does the prophet name?

5 Together
Talk together: Why do people often remember things a little differently, even when they saw the same thing?
Do together: Look at the same picture for ten seconds. Each of you writes down what you saw. What is the same, and what is different? You will probably remember the most important things alike.
