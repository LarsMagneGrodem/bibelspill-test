Anna and the women
Door 15 in the game. Read it yourself in the Bible: Luke 2:36–40.

3 From Luke
Anna, a prophet, was also in the temple. She was the daughter of Phanuel, of the tribe of Asher. She was very old, a widow of eighty-four. She never left the temple, but worshipped God night and day with fasting and prayer. Now she thanked God and spoke about the child to all who were waiting for God to set Jerusalem free (Luke 2:36–38).
Find the seven words from the story of Anna. They go across or down. ANNA, PROPHET, TEMPLE, PHANUEL, ASHER, WIDOW, PRAYER.

4 Detective: today’s clues
Today’s check: Women followed Jesus and helped him and the disciples with what they owned (Luke 8:1–3). The historians say: Strong clues. Here is why: Mark and Matthew also tell that women followed Jesus from Galilee and helped him. The Gospels name several of them, such as Mary Magdalene.
How did the women help Jesus and the disciples? They built boats for them / They helped them with what they owned / They wrote their books
Extra challenge: Look up Luke 8:2–3. Write the names of three women who followed Jesus.

5 Together
Talk together: Luke often tells of a man and a woman side by side, like Simeon and Anna. Why do you think he does that?
Do together: Who has told you about Jesus? Make a thank-you card for one of them.
