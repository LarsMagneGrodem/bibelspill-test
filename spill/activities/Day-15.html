Read the tasks as text
Picture descriptions for the tasks:
1. The picture shows Nora by a bench and a toy kite with two dots and two bows. A has one dot and two bows. B has two dots and one bow. C has two dots and two bows. Circle or say which one is the same as in the picture.
2. The cards from the left: Lea says Amir told me about the kite. Nora says I saw the kite land. Amir says Nora told me about the kite. Three empty boxes with arrows between them: say or write the names in the order the story was told.
3. The same three sentences are shown together by pictures of the people. Answer choices: One: Nora. Three: all of them.
4. An empty drawing frame. The cards do not say who owns the kite. Draw an owner you make up yourself.

Draw a kite on a separate sheet. Hide the sheet and describe the kite to a friend, who draws it. Look
