What Mary kept in her heart
Door 5 in the game. Read it yourself in the Bible: Luke 2:15–19.

3 From Luke
The shepherds hurried to Bethlehem and found Mary and Joseph, and the baby lying in the manger. They told what had been said to them about the child, and everyone who heard it was amazed. But Mary treasured all these things and pondered them in her heart (Luke 2:15–19).
Who did what? Draw a line from each dot on the left to a dot on the right. 1 The shepherds. 2 Those who heard it. 3 Mary. 4 The baby. A treasured it all in her heart. B lay in the manger. C were amazed. D told what they had heard about the child.

4 Detective: today’s clues
Today’s check: James led the Christians in Jerusalem (Acts 12:17; 15:13–21; 21:18). The historians say: Strong clues. Here is why: Paul met James in Jerusalem and counts him as one of the leaders there. The historian Josephus, who was not a Christian, also writes about James, “the brother of Jesus, who was called Christ”.
Two who write about James are Paul and Josephus. Which of them was not a Christian? Paul / Josephus / Neither of them
Extra challenge: Read Acts 21:17–18. Who did Paul and his companions visit in Jerusalem?

5 Together
Talk together: Luke writes that Mary kept everything she went through and thought about it. Who could she have told, and how could it have reached Luke?
Do together: Tell something from when you were small. Who told you the parts you cannot remember yourself? That is how families keep what has happened.
