Picture descriptions for the tasks:
1. The cards from the left: Mika, Ina, Lea. Each card has a dot below it for the arrows. Ina tells Mika. Mika tells Lea. Draw or describe the arrows.
2. Example: a star with one line under it. A: a leaf with one line. B: a star with no line. C: a star with one line. D: a star with two lines.
4. A letter with a big empty circle for your seal.
