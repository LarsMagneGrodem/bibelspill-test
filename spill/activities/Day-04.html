3 From Luke
The angel Gabriel was sent to a town in Galilee, to a virgin called Mary, pledged to be married to Joseph. The angel said she would have a son, Jesus. Mary asked, “How will this be?” The angel answered, and Mary said, “May it be to me as you have said” (Luke 1:26–38).
Write the answers. The letters in the thick boxes spell a town, from top to bottom. 1. Gabriel is an … (5). 2. The region where the town lies (7). 3. Mary’s relative, who was also expecting a child (9). 4. She was visited by the angel (4). 5. The name of the angel (7). 6. The name the child was to be given (5). 7. The child will be given King David’s … (6). 8. Mary was promised in marriage to … (6).

4 Detective: today’s clues
Today’s check: Jesus grew up in Nazareth, a small village in Galilee (Luke 1:26–27; 2:39, 51; 4:16). The historians say: Strong clues. Here is why: All four Gospels say so, and archaeologists have found a house from Jesus’ time in Nazareth. Nathanael even doubts that something good could come from a place like that.
Nathanael asked, “Can anything good come from Nazareth?” (John 1:46). What does that tell us about the town? That it was large and rich / That the king lived there / That it was small and little known
Extra challenge: Read John 1:45–46. Who answered Nathanael, and what did he say?

5 Together
Talk together: Mary asked the angel, “How will this happen?” What do you think about her asking questions?
Do together: Find Nazareth, Jerusalem and Bethlehem on a map in a Bible or an atlas. Which town is furthest north?
