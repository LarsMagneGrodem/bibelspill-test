Picture descriptions for the tasks:
1. Lea’s house is at the bottom left, the well in the middle and Mika’s house at the top right. Roads link the places. A tree stands at the top left, a bread stall at the bottom right. Tell a walk from Lea’s house, past the well and on to Mika.
2. The cards from the left: They share bread. Lea fetches water. Lea visits Mika. By each card there is an empty circle for the number.
4. A blank signpost with an arrow pointing right.

3
What do both say?
Lea and Mika talk about the same visit.
LEA
“I brought water from the well
to Mika.”
MIKA
“Lea had water with her when
she came.”
Put a cross by the sentence that fits with both.
Lea came without water.
Lea brought water.
4
Make a signpost
Show the way to the well with a drawing. Then even someone who cannot read can
understand the sign.
5
Be a living map
Make three stops in the room: house, well and friend. Ask a friend to walk the route. Give one
instruction at a time. Swap roles and make up a new walk.
