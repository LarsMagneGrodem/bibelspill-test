Disagreement at the temple
Door 19 in the game. Read it yourself in the Bible: Luke 20:27–40.

3 From Luke
Some Sadducees came to Jesus in the temple. They say there is no resurrection, and they asked him a hard question. Jesus answered that God is not the God of the dead, but of the living. Some of the teachers of the law said, “Well said, teacher” (Luke 20:27–40).
Luke tells what two groups believed (Acts 23:8). Write S for Sadducee or P for Pharisee in each box. “There are no angels.” “The dead will rise.” “There are both angels and spirits.” “The dead do not rise.”

4 Detective: today’s clues
Today’s check: The Sadducees did not believe that the dead will rise (Luke 20:27; Acts 23:8). The historians say: Strong clues. Here is why: Josephus knew the Sadducees and writes something that fits: they held that the soul dies with the body.
Several sources say the same about the Sadducees. Where do we find them? In the Bible / Outside the Bible: Luke’s Gospel. Mark’s Gospel. The historian Josephus.
Extra challenge: Read Acts 23:6–8. Which group does Paul say he belongs to?

5 Together
Talk together: Both Luke and the historian Josephus say that the Sadducees did not believe the dead will rise. Why is it useful when two sources say the same thing?
Do together: Find something you disagree about, such as the best sandwich filling. Practise listening to the end before you answer.
