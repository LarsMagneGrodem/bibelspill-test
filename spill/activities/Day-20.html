Picture descriptions for the tasks:
1. Two drawings of the same jetty: A has three baskets in a row. B has the same three and a fourth basket at the far right. The jetty, the boat with one sail and the rope from the boat to the jetty are otherwise the same. Find what is different and two things that are the same. Write or say the number of baskets in A and B.
2. The boat has four places for cargo. Three have a basket each. The fourth is empty. Draw a basket there, or say what you would put in the space. How many baskets did you add?

3 From Matthew and Luke
Matthew tells mostly about Joseph: an angel told him in a dream not to be afraid to take Mary home as his wife. Luke tells mostly about Mary. Both say that Joseph was from David’s family, and that Jesus was born in Bethlehem (Matthew 1:18–25; 2:1; Luke 1:27; 2:4–7).
Who tells what? Put one cross on each line. Luke / Matthew / Both: An angel comes to Mary. An angel speaks to Joseph in a dream. Jesus is born in Bethlehem. Shepherds come to the child. Wise men come to the child. Joseph is from David’s family.

4 Detective: today’s clues
Today’s check: Jesus was descended from King David (Luke 1:32; 2:4; 3:31). The historians say: Strong clues. Here is why: Paul writes it only about 25 years after Jesus’ death. Mark, Matthew and Revelation say it too. The family trees differ, and scholars discuss why. Both lead to David.
The two family trees have different names. What do both say all the same? That David lived in Nazareth / That Joseph was king / That Jesus was from David’s family
Extra challenge: Read Romans 1:3. What does Paul write about Jesus’ family?

5 Together
Talk together: If two friends wrote about the same school trip, what would make you more suspicious: that they wrote it word for word the same, or a little differently?
Do together: Make a small family tree: you, your parents and your grandparents. Who can tell you more about the family?
