The baskets on the jetty

Day 20 · Five optional activities. Get a pencil and some colours, and choose one task at a time.

Read the tasks as text
Picture descriptions for the tasks:
1. Two drawings of the same jetty: A has three baskets in a row. B has the same three and a fourth basket at the far right. The jetty, the boat with one sail and the rope from the boat to the jetty are otherwise the same. Find what is different and two things that are the same. Write or say the number of baskets in A and B.
2. The boat has four places for cargo. Three have a basket each. The fourth is empty. Draw a basket there, or say what you would put in the space. How many baskets did you add?
3. Lea says I counted three baskets. Mika says I counted four baskets. We do not know when they counted. Choose a useful question: Who has the neatest handwriting? Or: Did you count the baskets at the same time?
4. Two empty drawing frames marked A and B. Draw three things in A, and add or take away one thing in B.
