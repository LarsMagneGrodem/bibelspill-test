The priest who fell silent
Door 3 in the game. Read it yourself in the Bible: Luke 1:57–66.

3 From Luke
Elizabeth gave birth to a son. The neighbours and relatives wanted to call him Zechariah after his father. But Elizabeth said, “No! He is to be called John.” Zechariah asked for a writing tablet and wrote the same name. At once he could speak again, and he praised God (Luke 1:57–64).
What happened first? Write 1, 2, 3 and 4 in the circles. The neighbours want to call him Zechariah. Elizabeth gives birth to a son. Zechariah writes the name on a tablet. Zechariah can speak again. Write the name on the tablet.

4 Detective: today’s clues
Today’s check: The child John became a prophet who baptised people, and many listened to him (Luke 1:76–80; 3:2–3). The historians say: Strong clues. Here is why: The historian Josephus was a Jew, not a Christian. He too writes about John who baptised, and about the many who gathered round him.
Josephus also writes about John who baptised. Who was Josephus? A Jewish historian who was not a Christian / One of Jesus’ disciples / A Roman emperor
Extra challenge: Read Luke 1:65–66. What did people in the hill country do when they heard about this?

5 Together
Talk together: Luke tells us that Zechariah did not believe the angel and lost his voice. Why do you think Luke included something that makes a well-known priest look a bit silly?
Do together: Stay silent for two minutes, like Zechariah. Make the others understand a name only by writing or drawing.
