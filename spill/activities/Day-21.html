Read the tasks as text
Picture descriptions for the tasks:
1. The drawing shows a sun, a sailing boat with two round windows, a mast, one sail and waves. The copy shows a star instead of the sun and only the left of the two windows. The boat, the mast, the sail and the waves are otherwise the same. Find two changes and one thing that is the same.
2. Three cards with arrows from A to B and from B to C. A has two stars. B has three stars. C is empty. In B an extra star was added. C is copied from B. Draw or say how many stars C gets. Where did the extra star first appear: A or B?
3. The message says: Draw two circles and one star. Drawing A has three circles and one star. B has two circles and one star. C has two circles and two stars. Choose the drawing that follows the message.
4. Two empty frames marked A and B. Draw three shapes in A and copy them in B with A in view.
