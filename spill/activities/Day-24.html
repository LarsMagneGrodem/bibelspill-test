Read the tasks as text
Picture descriptions for the tasks:
1. On the drawn writing desk there are two scrolls on the left and a string in the middle. On the right is a lamp. Circle or point to the string. Count the scrolls and point to what can give light.
2. The top row has A a triangle, B two rings and C a cross. The bottom row has 1 a cross, 2 a triangle and 3 two rings. Connect matching marks with a line, or say the letter and number together.
3. The picture card The table now shows a string. Mira says: The string was on the table yesterday too. What does the picture show? A: The picture shows that the string is on the table now. B: The picture shows that the string was there yesterday.
4. An empty circle for your own seal and a frame where you can draw or write one thing you want to look into.
5. Choose one thing. Look at it together. One of you tells something about it. Make three boxes on a sheet: We see this. You tell us this. We wonder about this.
