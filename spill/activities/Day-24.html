The scroll is sealed
Door 24 in the game. Read it yourself in the Bible: Luke 1:1–4.

3 From Luke
Now the scroll is finished. Luke has built on those who were eyewitnesses from the beginning. He has looked carefully into everything and written it down in order, so that Theophilus would know how reliable the things he has been taught are (Luke 1:1–4). Now you have followed the clues yourself.
Do you remember the journey? Write the answers in the boxes. The letters in the thick boxes spell a word from the angels’ praise, from top to bottom. 1. They kept watch over their sheep at night (9). 2. The town where Jesus was born (9). 3. The king who was a shepherd boy near Bethlehem (5). 4. The priest who could not speak (9). 5. He took the child in his arms (6).

4 Detective: today’s clues
Today’s check: On Sunday morning the women found the tomb where Jesus had been laid, and it was empty (Luke 24:1–3). The historians say: Strong clues. Here is why: All four Gospels tell it, and the opponents said the body had been stolen. That takes for granted that the tomb was empty. Many scholars regard this as well supported.
The opponents said the body had been stolen. So what did they too take for granted? That the tomb was empty / That nobody knew where the tomb was / That the stone was still in place
Extra challenge: Look back over all 24 doors. Which clue do you remember best? Write or draw it on the back of the sheet.

5 Together
Talk together: Go through Luke’s four questions together. Which door do you remember best, and which question helped you there?
Do together: Read the whole Christmas story aloud together: Luke 2:1–20. Merry Christmas!
