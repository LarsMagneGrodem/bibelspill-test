Read the tasks as text
Picture descriptions for the tasks:
1. Left scrolls from the top: 1 I shared my bread. 2 You got a place at the table. 3 We fetched water for you. Right scrolls from the top: A You were thirsty. We gave you a drink. B You were hungry. We gave you food. C You were alone. We sat together. The left scrolls have small drawings of bread, a bench and a cup. Connect or tell which lines go together.
2. A star means clap, a circle means pat your knees, a line means pause. Example: star, circle, circle, line. A: star, circle, star, line. B: star, circle, circle, line. C: circle, star, circle, line.
4. A blank drawing frame with a small drawn heart and musical notes in the corner.
