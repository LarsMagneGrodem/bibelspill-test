Mary’s song
Door 6 in the game. Read it yourself in the Bible: Luke 1:46–55.

3 From Luke
Mary visited her relative Elizabeth in the hill country. There she sang a song of praise to God: he has been mindful of his servant. He has lifted up the humble and filled the hungry with good things. He has remembered what he promised to Abraham (Luke 1:46–55).
Who is helped in Mary’s song, and who is brought down? Put one cross on each line. Is helped / Is brought down: The humble (verse 52). The rulers on their thrones (verse 52). The hungry (verse 53). The rich (verse 53). The proud (verse 51). Draw something you want to thank God for.

4 Detective: today’s clues
Today’s check: Mary’s song of praise is older than Luke’s book. Luke wrote down a song that already existed (Luke 1:46–55). The historians say: Good clues. Here is why: The song is like Hannah’s song and Israel’s old psalms. Many scholars think it is older than Luke’s book. That fits what Luke tells: that Mary sang it.
Which song in the Old Testament is like Mary’s song? Hannah’s song / Noah’s song / Goliath’s song
Extra challenge: Read Hannah’s song in 1 Samuel 2:1–8. Find one thing both Hannah and Mary sing about.

5 Together
Talk together: Mary’s song is like Hannah’s song in the Old Testament. Who does God help in both songs?
Do together: Make your own song of thanks. Each person says one line that begins with “Thank you for …”. Clap a rhythm to it.
