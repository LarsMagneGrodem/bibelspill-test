Two views of a picnic

Day 13 · Five optional activities. Get a pencil and some colours, and choose one task at a time.

Download both sheets · PDFAnswers for adults · PDF
Read the tasks as text
Picture descriptions for the tasks:
1. A is Mina’s picture, B is Noah’s picture. Both have a big tree, a striped blanket and two cups. A also has a bird on a small branch. B also has a kite with a tail. Find three things that are the same; circle, point or tell.
2. Mina’s card: We sat on a striped blanket under the tree. Noah’s card: We drank from two cups under the tree. Underline or point to the words that say where they were.
3. New cards: Mina says We drank from the cups. Noah says It was warm outside. Answer choices: They drank water. They drank hot chocolate. The cards do not say what they drank.
4. A big empty drawing frame with small leaves in the corners.

Make up something fun that happens after the picnic. Draw it in the frame. It is your story!

Two artists – one small collection
Put four things on the table. Each of you draws two of them without showing the other. Put the
drawings side by side. What did you both draw? What did only one of you draw?
