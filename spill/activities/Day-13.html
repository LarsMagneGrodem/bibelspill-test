Two accounts
Door 13 in the game. Read it yourself in the Bible: Matthew 2:1–12.

3 From Matthew
Matthew tells of another visit. Wise men from the east, learned men also called Magi, came to Jerusalem and asked for the one who had been born king of the Jews. King Herod was disturbed. The star went ahead of them to Bethlehem. There they went into the house, saw the child and presented gifts of gold, frankincense and myrrh (Matthew 2:1–12).
Two visits to the same child. Write S for the shepherds (Luke 2) or W for the wise men (Matthew 2) in each box. An angel told them. They came from the fields nearby. They came from far away, from the east. They brought gifts. They found the baby in a manger. They followed a star.

4 Detective: today’s clues
Today’s check: Jesus was born while Herod the Great was king in Judea (Luke 1:5, 26, 36; Matt 2:1). The historians say: Strong clues. Here is why: Matthew and Luke each wrote his own account, but both say Herod. Josephus tells when Herod lived and died.
Matthew and Luke each wrote his own account. What do they agree on? That the shepherds brought gold / That the wise men came to the fields / That Jesus was born in Bethlehem while Herod was king
Extra challenge: Read Matthew 2:1–12 carefully. How many wise men were there?

5 Together
Talk together: Matthew tells of the learned men from the East, Luke of the shepherds. Is that a contradiction, or could both have happened?
Do together: Each of you tells about something you did together, a trip or a birthday. What did only one of you include?
