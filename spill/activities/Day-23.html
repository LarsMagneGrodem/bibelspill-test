A Messiah who dies?
Door 23 in the game. Read it yourself in the Bible: Luke 24:1–12.

3 From Luke
Jesus was crucified at the place called the Skull and laid in a tomb. Very early on the first day of the week, Sunday, the women came to the tomb. The stone had been rolled away, and they did not find the body of Jesus. Two men in shining clothes said, “He is not here; he has risen.” The women told everything to the apostles (Luke 23:33, 53; 24:1–10).
Luke gives the women’s names (Luke 24:10). Find the seven words. They go across or down. MAGDALENE, JOANNA, MARY, PETER, TOMB, STONE, SUNDAY.

4 Detective: today’s clues
Today’s check: Jesus was crucified outside Jerusalem while Pontius Pilate governed Judea (Luke 23:1, 33, 46; 24:20). The historians say: Strong clues. Here is why: The Roman historian Tacitus writes that Christ was put to death on Pilate’s sentence. Josephus says that Pilate condemned him to the cross. All four Gospels say the same.
Tacitus writes that Christ was put to death while Pilate governed. Who was Tacitus? One of the twelve disciples / A Roman historian / A priest in the temple
Extra challenge: Read 1 Corinthians 15:3–5. Paul passes on something he himself received. Which four things does he say about Christ?

5 Together
Talk together: Paul wrote that Jesus died, was buried and rose again. Why does it matter that this was told while the eyewitnesses were still alive?
Do together: Light a candle. Read Luke 24:1–6 aloud to each other. What do you think the women were thinking on the way back?
