Read the tasks as text
Picture descriptions for the tasks:
1. The text says: Mika promised to help Lea with the plants. But he went home. Later he came back to help. Three cards stand in this order from the left: A Mika goes home. B Mika comes back. C Mika promises to help. There is an empty circle under each picture. Write or say 1, 2 and 3 in the right order.
2. Lea’s tray on the left has three pots with a plant in each. The tray on the right has the same plants on the left and right, but the middle space is empty. Draw or describe what is missing to make the trays the same.
3. Mika’s note says: I promised to help Lea. But I went home. What does the note tell us? A: Mika says that he went home. B: Mika says why he went home.
4. Two empty comic boxes: What can he say? What can he do? Make up a new start for Mika.
