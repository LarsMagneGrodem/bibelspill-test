Read the tasks as text
Picture descriptions for the tasks:
1. The house is open towards us. On the left are two mats; a lamp hangs on the wall above them. On the right is a feed box with hay. A doorway links the rooms. Under the house are the cards SLEEP, FOOD FOR ANIMALS and LIGHT. Connect the cards to the things, or tell what fits.
2. The note says: In the room there were two mats and a lamp. A has two mats along the left wall, the lamp on the right and a round window. B has two mats at the bottom, the lamp at the top in the middle and a square window. Answer choices: Only A, Only B, Both.
3. The new note says: The guest slept on a mat. Answer choices: The guest slept on a mat., with a small picture of a mat. There was a cat in the room., with a small picture of a cat. The pictures are drawn for the answer choices.
4. A big empty frame with room walls and a small star as decoration.

Build and give clues
Build a small room from blocks or card. Name two things a friend should put in. Let your friend make
up one more thing. Point together: What did you ask for, and what did your friend make up?
