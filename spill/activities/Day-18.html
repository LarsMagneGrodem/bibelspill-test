Hope and clues in the garden

Day 18 · Five optional activities. Get a pencil and some colours, and choose one task at a time.

Download both sheets · PDFAnswers for adults · PDF
Read the tasks as text
Picture descriptions for the tasks:
1. The garden shows A: a bench by a tree. B: a tap marked DRINKING WATER. C: a pot with flowers. Mira hopes to rest her legs. Mika hopes to have a drink of water. Lea hopes to see flowers. Write or say A, B or C by each card.
2. The lines: I hope the sun comes out. I saw three flowers in a pot. I hope my friend comes. Choose HOPING or HAVE SEEN for each line.
3. Mira says I hope five flowers will grow in the pot. Mika says I saw three flowers in the pot. Picture A has five flowers in one pot. Picture B has three flowers in one pot. Choose the picture that fits what Mika says he saw.
4. An empty frame with a small leaf. Draw something you wish there were in a garden.
