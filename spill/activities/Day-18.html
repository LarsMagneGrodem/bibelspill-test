What people expected
Door 18 in the game. Read it yourself in the Bible: Luke 4:16–30.

3 From Luke
In the synagogue in his home town of Nazareth, Jesus read from the prophet Isaiah. Then he said, “Today this scripture is fulfilled.” At first people were amazed at his words. But when he said more, they became furious and drove him out of the town (Luke 4:16–30).
What did Jesus read (Luke 4:18)? Write the words from the word bank in the right places. Jesus read about good news for the (1) …, freedom for the (2) … and sight for the (3) …. Then he rolled up the (4) … and sat down. Word bank: prisoners, poor, blind, scroll. Draw a scroll.

4 Detective: today’s clues
Today’s check: When Jesus spoke in the synagogue in his home town of Nazareth, the people there rejected him (Luke 4:16–30). The historians say: Strong clues. Here is why: Mark and Matthew tell it too, and John knows the saying that a prophet is not honoured in his own home town.
How many Gospels tell that Jesus was rejected in his home town? One: only Luke / Three: Mark, Matthew and Luke / None
Extra challenge: Read Luke 4:24. What does Jesus say about a prophet?

5 Together
Talk together: Why do you think it was hard for the people in his home town to believe in him?
Do together: Read Luke 4:18–19 aloud. Who needs good news today? Do one good thing for one of them.
