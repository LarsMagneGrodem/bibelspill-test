For adults: answers and printing

Open the answers and small hints (12 pages). Choose A4 and “fit to page” when you print. The colours are decoration; the answers do not require the child to tell colours apart. Try one day first.

Read the two cards together if the child needs help. Let the child point and explain. Drawing and playing together have no answers.

These are made-up exercises inspired by the themes of the game, not new historical sources. They practise noticing details and telling apart what we see, what someone tells us and what we still wonder about.
