Answers and printing

Open the answers, small hints and group guide (17 pages). Choose A4 and “fit to page” when you print. The colours are decoration; the answers do not require the child to tell colours apart. Try one day first.

Read the text together if the child needs help. Let the child point and explain. Using the sheets in Sunday school or with a class? The answer booklet begins with a short guide for groups, with questions to talk about for each week.

Page 1 of each sheet has made-up exercises inspired by the themes of the game. They practise noticing details and telling apart what we see and what someone tells us. Page 2 follows today’s door: a text from Luke, a task on the text and “Today’s check” from the game. Luke writes that he looked carefully into everything (Luke 1:3), and the sheets let children see the clues for themselves.
