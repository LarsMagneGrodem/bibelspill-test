The priest in the temple
Door 2 in the game. Read it yourself in the Bible: Luke 1:5–25.

3 From Luke
The priest Zechariah and his wife Elizabeth had no children, and they were both very old. One day Zechariah was chosen by lot to burn incense inside the temple. There an angel told him they would have a son, John. Zechariah did not believe the words, and he became silent. The people waited outside and wondered (Luke 1:5–22).
Find the seven words from the story. They go across or down. ZECHARIAH, ELIZABETH, JOHN, TEMPLE, INCENSE, ANGEL, SILENT.

4 Detective: today’s clues
Today’s check: The priests served in fixed shifts, and lots were drawn to decide who would burn the incense (Luke 1:5, 8–9). The historians say: Strong clues. Here is why: The Old Testament and the historian Josephus tell of the same priestly shifts. Later Jewish rules tell of the drawing of lots for the incense.
Three clues tell of the priests’ shifts and the drawing of lots. Where do we find them? In the Bible / Outside the Bible: 1 Chronicles. The historian Josephus. The Mishnah, the Jewish rules.
Extra challenge: Read Luke 1:21–22. What could the people outside see for themselves? What did someone have to tell them afterwards?

5 Together
Talk together: The people outside the temple saw that Zechariah could not speak when he came out. What could they not know about what happened inside?
Do together: One of you leaves the room. The others do one small thing. Come back in: what can you see for yourself, and what must someone tell you?
