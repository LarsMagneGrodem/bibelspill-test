Read the tasks as text
Picture descriptions for the tasks:
1. On the counter: a small basket on the left, a jar in the middle and a wide basket on the right. Under the counter: a jar on the left, a tall basket in the middle and three apples on the right. Count only the baskets.
2. The cards from the left: Write the number. (the number 3 on a sheet). Count once more. (three baskets with an arrow above). Count the baskets. (three baskets). Under each card is an empty circle for the number.
4. A big empty basket with five empty boxes below. Draw things in the basket and one mark for each thing in the boxes.

From message to note

Made-up exercises inspired by the game. These are not historical sources.
2/2
3
What do the notes say?
Read both notes. Which one says that someone has counted?
A · MESSAGE
“All the baskets must be counted.”
Note A
B · NOTE
“We have counted three baskets.”
Note B
A message to do something is not the same as a note saying that it has been done.
4
Fill your own basket
Draw five different things in the basket. Make a small mark for each thing in the boxes below.
5
Count and check together
Put some small things on a table. Count them on your own, one each. Write down the number
without showing it. Compare. Did you get different answers? Count together and find out why.
