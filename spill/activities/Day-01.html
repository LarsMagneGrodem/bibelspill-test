The accident in the square
Door 1 in the game. Read it yourself in the Bible: Luke 1:1–4.

3 From Luke
Luke begins his book by telling how he worked. Many had set out to tell what had happened. He built on those who were eyewitnesses from the beginning, and he looked carefully into everything. Then he wrote it down in order for a man called Theophilus (Luke 1:1–4).
Luke wrote in Greek. Use the key and write our letters in the boxes. Which two names are these? Key: Α = A, Ε = E, Θ = TH, Ι = I, Κ = K, Λ = L, Ο = O, Σ = S, Υ = U, Φ = F. ΛΟΥΚΑΣ ΘΕΟΦΙΛΟΣ.

4 Detective: today’s clues
Today’s check: Before Luke wrote, others had already written down what happened with Jesus (Luke 1:1). The historians say: Strong clues. Here is why: Most scholars think Mark’s Gospel is older than Luke’s, and that Luke uses much of it. Then what Luke writes holds: others had written before him.
Which book do most scholars think Luke used when he wrote? John’s Gospel / Mark’s Gospel / The Acts of the Apostles
Extra challenge: Look up Mark 2:1–12 and Luke 5:17–26. Find two things that are the same in the two accounts.

5 Together
Talk together: Who would you rather ask about what happened in the market square: someone who saw it, or someone who heard about it from others? Why?
Do together: Play the whispering game: whisper a sentence from ear to ear. What reached the last person? Who should you have asked?
