Light for more nations
Door 22 in the game. Read it yourself in the Bible: Luke 2:29–32.

3 From Luke
Simeon called the child “a light for revelation to the nations” (Luke 2:32). Later Luke tells about Cornelius, a Roman officer in Caesarea. He was not a Jew, but he prayed to God and gave gifts to the poor. Peter was in Joppa. He was fetched to Cornelius’ house and told about Jesus there (Acts 10).
Follow Peter from Joppa to Cornelius’ house in Caesarea. You cannot go through the walls. JOPPA – CAESAREA.

4 Detective: today’s clues
Today’s check: There were non-Jews who prayed to the God of Israel and gave to the poor, as the officer Cornelius did (Acts 10:1–2). The historians say: Strong clues. Here is why: Josephus, who lived in Luke’s time, tells of people who were not Jews but worshipped the God of Israel. A stone from a later time has a whole list of such “God-fearers”.
What do we call those who were not Jews, but worshipped the God of Israel? God-fearers / Sadducees / Tax collectors
Extra challenge: Read Acts 10:1–2. Write two things Luke tells about Cornelius.

5 Together
Talk together: Simeon called Jesus a light for the nations. How do we see that in the story of Cornelius?
Do together: Learn to say “Merry Christmas” in another language. Who nearby can teach you?
