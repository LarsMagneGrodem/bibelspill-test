Light around the same table

Day 22 · Five optional activities. Get a pencil and some colours, and choose one task at a time.

Download both sheets · PDFAnswers for adults · PDF
Read the tasks as text
Picture descriptions for the tasks:
1. A made-up courtyard with three houses and paths that meet at a round table. There is a lamp in the window of the house on the left, one on the table and one on the bench on the right. Two loaves of bread also lie on the table. Find the three lamps and point to the table.
2. The word list says: light = lys, water = vann, bread = brød. On the left are LIGHT, WATER and BREAD. On the right are VANN, BRØD and LYS. Connect the words that mean the same.
3. Mira writes: Ta med lys. Ben writes: Bring a light. The word list says that Ta med lys means Bring a light. Picture A shows a loaf of bread. Picture B shows a lamp. Choose the picture that fits both messages.
4. An empty sign to draw a welcome picture on.
