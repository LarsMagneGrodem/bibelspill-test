Read the tasks as text
Picture descriptions for the tasks:
1. The map shows a gate on the left, a well in the middle and a bench on the right. Card 1: Start at the gate. Card 2: Go on to the bench. Card 3: Last of all, go to the well. Draw arrows or say the order.
2. Mika’s bag has a star and two stripes. A has a star and one stripe. B has a star and two stripes. C has a dot and two stripes. Circle or say the letter.
3. Mira’s first card: I looked for Mika by the gate. I did not find him there. Next card: After that, I found Mika by the well. Answer choices: By the gate. By the well.
4. An empty drawing frame with a star. Make up and draw a clear meeting place.
