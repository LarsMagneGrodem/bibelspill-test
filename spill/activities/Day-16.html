Twelve years old in the temple
Door 16 in the game. Read it yourself in the Bible: Luke 2:41–52.

3 From Luke
When Jesus was twelve years old, the family went to the Passover festival in Jerusalem. On the way home his parents thought he was among the travellers. After a day’s journey they began to search. After three days they found him in the temple, among the teachers. He said, “Didn’t you know I had to be in my Father’s house?” (Luke 2:41–49).
The numbers are in the text above. Write them in the boxes and add them up. Jesus was … years old. His parents travelled … day before they began to search. They found him after … days. Add the three numbers. Which door is it today? Draw the road from Nazareth to Jerusalem.

4 Detective: today’s clues
Today’s check: Jesus’ family went to Jerusalem for Passover with a large company from Galilee (Luke 2:41–44). The historians say: Strong clues. Here is why: Josephus tells of large companies from Galilee going to the feasts in Jerusalem. John tells that Jesus’ family did so too.
Why did his parents not notice at once that Jesus was missing (Luke 2:44)? They travelled in a large company and thought he was with relatives and friends / They had forgotten him / It was dark all day
Extra challenge: Read Luke 2:46–47. What did Jesus do among the teachers in the temple?

5 Together
Talk together: What was Jesus doing when his parents found him in the temple?
Do together: Ask each other three good questions about something you wonder about, as Jesus asked the teachers.
